# POS cashier guide

## Start of shift

1. Sign in using your own account and staff OTP.
2. Confirm the branch shown is your physical shop.
3. Open `/pos-desk/` and start a shift with the counted opening cash.
4. Do not sell before the shift is open or use another cashier's session.

## Complete a walk-in sale

1. Search by product name or SKU and select the exact variant/colour/size.
2. Confirm available branch stock and build the cart.
3. Read back the items and show the customer-facing total when appropriate.
4. Add customer phone only for the receipt/approved purpose.
5. For cash, enter the amount received; confirm calculated change before submission.
6. For Paybill/manual evidence, follow the manager verification process. A typed code or screenshot is not proof.
7. Confirm once. Wait for the successful sale/receipt state; do not double-click or rebuild the sale while processing.
8. Give the receipt and correct change. Stock is deducted by the completed transaction.

## Discounts and corrections

Cashiers cannot self-authorize discounts. The manager must review the cart, enter their own full password and provide a reason. Do not ask for or remember a manager credential.

If a completed sale is wrong, do not edit or delete it. Start the void/reversal workflow; an authorized manager supplies their password and reason. A sale from a closed shift uses the controlled return/correction path rather than reopening drawer history.

## Close shift

1. Stop new sales and count physical cash away from customers.
2. Enter closing cash and review expected cash, variance, payment split and sales list.
3. Explain any material variance truthfully. Variances above KES 50 enter manager review.
4. Submit once and secure cash using the branch procedure.

Report immediately: duplicate receipt/order, wrong branch, unavailable stock that sold, negative/incorrect change, failed Paybill verification, missing SMS with completed sale, or inability to reconcile cash.
