# Rider operating guide

Riders use individual staff accounts with a branch-bound rider profile. Never share login/OTP or accept an assignment outside the application.

1. Sign in, complete staff OTP and mark availability only when ready for work.
2. Open your assigned queue at `/api/logistics/my-deliveries/`; it must show only your assignments.
3. Before pickup, compare order number and packed parcel with staff. Do not expose customer details beyond delivery need.
4. Record physical pickup/dispatch only after taking custody.
5. Follow the allowed sequence: assigned → picked up/dispatched → out for delivery → delivered, or the documented failure/return path.
6. Never mark delivered before handover. The customer may confirm or dispute separately.
7. For failure, record the correct operational status and contact the branch. Do not cancel the order, refund money or alter inventory yourself.
8. Protect the phone/session and customer address. Do not photograph payment messages or share customer data through personal groups.

Report lost parcel/device, wrong address, unsafe delivery, suspected fraud, cash discrepancy, customer dispute or inability to return goods immediately. Manager overrides require a reason and audit trail.

Route optimization, live public GPS maps and offline rider operation are not part of the MVP.
