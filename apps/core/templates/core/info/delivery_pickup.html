S18 pickup

Online checkout is for pickup at second floor, S18, Gaberone Plaza, unless our staff directs you otherwise. Paid items are held for a maximum of two working days.

Want it sent to your town?

Choose “Send to my town” in checkout and enter the recipient, county, town and preferred collection area. This creates a quote request only—no order, payment or stock reservation. During Monday–Saturday opening hours, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, we aim to confirm the operator, exact collection point, transport fee and expected arrival within 30 working minutes.

The quote is valid for 24 hours. We recheck product prices, discounts, coupons and stock before you accept the final total. Only acceptance creates a prepaid parcel order and reserves available items. WhatsApp remains available for help if the response is delayed.

Before leaving

Check the product, size, colour and condition with our staff before leaving. Keep your official payment confirmation available for verification.
