1. Who these terms apply to

These terms apply when you browse Emporium Plug’s website, create a customer account, place an online order, arrange a town parcel, request support, submit a review or request a return. “Emporium Plug”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the business operating the Emporium Plug retail service. The approved legal business name and service address must be inserted before launch.

2. Accounts and customer information

You must provide accurate contact information and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity performed through your account unless you promptly report suspected unauthorised access. Guest checkout may be available with a valid phone number. We may temporarily restrict an account to protect customers, investigate misuse or comply with law.

3. Product information, availability and prices

We aim to describe products, options, images and prices accurately. Screen colours, packaging and minor manufacturer changes can differ from photographs. Availability is rechecked before checkout, and stock can change while an item is in a cart. A cart does not reserve stock. If a price or availability changes before confirmation, we will show or communicate the updated position so you can decide whether to continue.

4. Placing and accepting an order

You must review the products, quantities, fulfilment choice and final amount before confirming. An order acknowledgement records your request; it does not prove payment or guarantee acceptance. We may refuse or cancel an order for unavailable stock, an obvious pricing error, suspected fraud, an invalid contact, a payment mismatch or another lawful operational reason. If confirmed money must be returned, staff will record and reconcile the outcome through the approved process.

5. Payments

Supported scenarios may include M-Pesa STK Push, an approved Paybill/manual-reference flow, or cash on approved collection or walk-in sales. Never share your M-Pesa PIN with Emporium Plug staff or enter it anywhere except the official prompt on your phone. A typed reference, screenshot or forwarded message is not proof of payment. Payment is confirmed only after an authorised provider response or an authorised and audited staff verification. Partial, excess, duplicate or unidentified payments require reconciliation and do not automatically complete an order.

6. S18 pickup

Unless we tell you otherwise, customer pickup is from second floor, S18, Gaberone Plaza. Bring the order details and official payment confirmation. Paid items are normally held for two working days after the ready notice. If you cannot collect in time, contact us before the reservation expires. The authorised collector may be asked to provide the pickup code or other reasonable verification. Inspect the correct product, size, colour, quantity and condition before leaving.

7. Parcels to Kenyan towns

Automated doorstep delivery is not currently offered through public checkout. Town parcels are arranged with staff on WhatsApp. Before payment, confirm the destination town, exact collection point, recipient details, transport operator where applicable, fee and expected arrival. Travel time is an estimate and may be affected by the operator, traffic, weather or events outside our reasonable control. Collection may require identification or a code set by the transport operator.

8. Promotions and coupons

A promotion may have dates, minimum spend, usage limits and product, category or branch restrictions. Unless expressly stated, promotions cannot be exchanged for cash or combined. We may reject a code that is expired, exhausted, inapplicable, duplicated or used contrary to its published conditions.

9. Returns, defects and refunds

Return eligibility depends on the product, reason, condition, quantity, timing and proof of purchase. Eligible non-clothing goods may be submitted for review within seven days; submission is not automatic approval. Clothing, size and colour must be checked before leaving pickup where reasonably possible. Incorrect, damaged or defective delivered items should be reported promptly. Approved refunds are currently processed and reconciled manually; we do not claim an automated refund service. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

10. Reviews and acceptable use

Only verified purchasers may review a product. Reviews must reflect genuine experience and must not contain threats, hate, unlawful material, personal data about others, spam or misleading claims. We may moderate content for those reasons while preserving honest criticism. You must not attack, scrape abusively, interfere with, reverse engineer or attempt unauthorised access to the service.

11. Service availability

We work to keep the service available and information current, but maintenance, communications failures and external providers can interrupt it. If online checkout is unavailable, contact support before sending payment. We will not use this clause to avoid responsibilities that the law does not allow us to exclude.

12. Privacy and account deletion

Our privacy notice explains what information is used and how to request access, correction, restriction, export or deletion/anonymisation. Deleting an account does not necessarily erase transaction, payment, fraud-prevention or audit evidence that must still be retained for a lawful purpose. See Privacy and personal data.

Material changes will be posted with a revised date and, where appropriate, brought to account holders’ attention. These terms are intended to be governed by the laws of Kenya, subject to mandatory consumer and data-protection rights. For order help call 0739 150 246, 0116 153 335 or 0783 025 355. A formal legal notice address and dispute-contact email must be approved before launch.
