Where do I collect a pickup order?

Second floor, S18, Gaberone Plaza, unless our staff directs you otherwise.

How long will you hold my paid item?

Up to two working days. Please contact us early if you have a collection problem.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. We arrange delivery and parcel fulfilment across Kenya; timing and fees depend on the destination.

Why do I not choose a shop?

Emporium Plug is one company. We assign the shop that can fulfil your complete basket, so you can focus on the products and delivery choice.

What should I bring for pickup?

Bring the product photo or order details and the official payment confirmation. Inspect size, colour and condition before leaving.
