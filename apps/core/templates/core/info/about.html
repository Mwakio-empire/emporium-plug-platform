About Emporium Plug

Quality, variety and value—served with accountability.

Emporium Plug is a growing Nairobi wholesale and retail brand. We operate as one business across our physical locations, while the online store gives customers one clear place to browse and order.

How our shops work

Stock is received through our OTC operation and distributed to our Gaberone Plaza shops. The system manages branch stock behind the scenes, so online customers do not need to choose or understand an internal branch before shopping.

Pickup and town parcels

Public online checkout currently supports pickup from second floor, S18, Gaberone Plaza. If you need an item sent to another Kenyan town, our team arranges the collection point, transport fee and expected arrival with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

What customers can expect

  • Clear totals: the final payable amount is shown before an online order is confirmed.
  • Payment checks: typing an M-Pesa reference is never treated as proof of payment by itself.
  • Stock accountability: sales and stock movements are recorded against the responsible shop and staff account.
  • Human support: customers can speak to our team on 0739 150 246, 0116 153 335 or 0783 025 355.

Shop with confidence

For pickup, keep your official order or payment message and inspect the item’s size, colour and condition before leaving. Paid pickup items are normally held for two working days. Product-specific return eligibility is explained on our returns page.
