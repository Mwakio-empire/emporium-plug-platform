Privacy notice

Your information and choices

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This notice explains how {{ controller_name }} uses personal information to operate Emporium Plug’s shop, customer accounts, orders, payments, pickup, support and fraud controls.

{% if not privacy_notice_approved %}

1. Who controls your information

The data controller is {{ controller_name }}. The approved legal name, physical/postal address, ODPC registration position and responsible privacy contact must be configured before production. {% if privacy_contact %}Privacy email: {{ privacy_contact }}.{% else %}Until a dedicated privacy email is approved, customers can use the support numbers shown in the footer.{% endif %}

2. Information we use

  • Name, verified phone number, optional email and account status.
  • Cart, order items, prices, coupon use, pickup or parcel details and support notes.
  • M-Pesa phone, request/receipt reference, expected and received amount—never your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Reviews, return requests, product preferences you actively submit and communications.
  • Login, device/session, IP address, security, staff-action, payment and stock audit evidence.

3. Where it comes from

  • Directly from you during signup, checkout, pickup, returns or support.
  • From payment and communications providers when they report a transaction or delivery result.
  • From authorised staff when they process a sale, verify collection or record support.
  • Automatically from essential security/session activity when you use the site.

4. Why we use it

Purchases and requested services: account creation, checkout, payment, stock reservation, pickup, parcel arrangements, returns and customer support.

Legal and accounting duties: payment reconciliation, transaction records, tax/accounting support and valid data-subject requests.

Security and legitimate operations: prevent fraud, protect accounts, investigate disputes, enforce branch permissions and maintain accountable audit records.

Consent where required: optional direct marketing or non-essential analytics must remain off unless the required choice and withdrawal controls are implemented.

We do not ask you to “consent” to processing that is necessary to complete the order you requested. We explain that processing and use the lawful basis approved for the production notice. Optional marketing or tracking must be separate from checkout.

5. Sharing and processors

Information is available only to staff who need it for their role and may be processed by configured hosting/database, media storage, Safaricom/Daraja, SMS, approved WhatsApp and error-monitoring providers. We do not sell customer personal data. Provider contracts, hosting regions and safeguards for any processing outside Kenya must be approved and recorded before production. A transport operator may receive the recipient and collection details needed for a customer-approved town parcel.

6. Cookies and local session data

This version of the site uses essential first-party cookies only. They keep your cart and sign-in session working and protect forms against forgery. They are not advertising cookies and are not used to follow you across unrelated websites.

Cookie/dataPurposeTypical duration
sessionidSign-in, security state and the session cart.Up to 12 hours, or until cleared/logged out.
csrftokenProtects forms and account actions from cross-site request forgery.Browser-controlled security lifetime.

Because these cookies are necessary for a service you request, showing an “accept all” button would suggest a choice that the current checkout cannot operate without. If analytics, advertising pixels or other optional cookies are introduced later, they must remain disabled until the customer makes an informed choice, with an equally easy rejection and withdrawal route.

7. Retention and security

Information should be kept only for an approved business or legal purpose. Account details, guest orders, payment records, audit/security logs, notifications, returns, reviews and backups need a client-approved retention schedule before launch. When a record is no longer required it should be deleted, anonymised, pseudonymised or securely disposed of. Some transaction and audit evidence may need to be retained after an account closes.

The implemented safeguards include password hashing, OTP expiry, rate limits, role and branch restrictions, secure production cookies, audit trails and restricted exports. No internet service can promise absolute security; suspected incidents should be reported promptly through the approved privacy contact.

8. Your rights and account deletion

Subject to Kenyan law and identity verification, you may ask to be informed, access linked information, correct inaccurate data, object to or restrict processing, receive an available machine-readable export, or request erasure of information that is no longer lawfully required. An erasure request may result in deletion, anonymisation, restriction or a reasoned refusal where evidence must be retained for a legal obligation or claim.

Account deletion is therefore a reviewed, password-confirmed request—not an immediate destruction of orders, payment evidence or immutable audit records. We will record the outcome and reason. Complaints may also be made to Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner after using the appropriate process.

9. Children, automated decisions and changes

The service is not intentionally designed to collect children’s data without an approved age and guardian process. The current storefront does not make a solely automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects for a customer. We may update this notice when providers or features change; the revised date and material changes should be communicated appropriately.
